Black screen but the phone is still on: is it the screen?

Your phone rings, buzzes and plays notification sounds, but the screen stays black. Before you panic, try these quick fixes. If they don't work, here's how to tell if it's the screen.

How to tell your phone is still on

A black screen doesn't always mean a dead phone. Your phone is probably still on if:

  • It rings or vibrates when you call it from another phone
  • You hear notification sounds, or it vibrates when you plug in the charger
  • Siri or "Hey Google" answers when you talk to it
  • Your computer recognizes it when you plug it in with a cable

If the phone is clearly on but the screen stays black, the problem is usually the display, or a setting that made the screen look black. Here's how to find out which.

5 things to try first

1. Force restart

A frozen screen is often fixed with a force restart. It doesn't erase anything.

  • iPhone 8 and newer: press and release volume up, press and release volume down, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears (this can take 10–20 seconds).
  • Samsung Galaxy: hold the side button and volume down together for about 10 seconds until it restarts.

2. Charge it for at least 30 minutes

A very low battery can cause strange behavior. Use a charger and cable you know work, wait at least 30 minutes, and then try the force restart again. If your phone also has charging problems, see our guide: phone won't charge? 7 things to check.

3. Turn the brightness up with your voice

If the brightness got turned all the way down, the screen can look completely black. Say "Hey Siri, turn brightness all the way up" (iPhone) or use your Samsung or Google voice assistant to do the same.

4. Check for "Screen Curtain" (iPhone)

On iPhone, a VoiceOver feature called Screen Curtain turns the display black on purpose while the phone keeps working. If your iPhone is talking to you or reading things out loud, this may be it. Tap the screen three times quickly with three fingers to turn Screen Curtain off, or say "Hey Siri, turn off VoiceOver."

5. Remove the case and screen protector

Rarely, a case presses on the screen or buttons and causes problems. Take it off and try a force restart again.

The flashlight test

This quick test helps show what's wrong, especially on iPhones that have an LCD screen (iPhone 11, XR and SE models):

  1. Make sure the phone is on, then wake it by pressing the side button.
  2. Shine a flashlight onto the screen at an angle, very close.
  3. If you can faintly see icons or the lock screen, the display itself works but its backlight has failed. That's a hardware problem and needs a repair.

Most newer iPhones and Galaxy phones have OLED screens, which don't have a backlight. On those, a black screen while the phone is on usually means the screen itself is damaged. Not sure which screen your phone has? See which iPhones have LCD or OLED screens.

Common causes of a black screen

  • A drop, even without a visible crack. The display under the glass can break while the glass looks fine. Lines, flickering or a growing black spot before it went black are typical signs.
  • A loose display connection. A hard drop can loosen the cable that connects the screen inside the phone.
  • Liquid damage. Water that gets inside can damage the screen or its connection, sometimes hours or days later.
  • A software freeze, which a force restart usually fixes.
  • A problem with the phone's main board. This is less common, and a technician can check for it.

Good sign: if the phone still rings, vibrates and connects to your computer, the rest of the phone is most likely working. In that case, a screen replacement usually brings it back.

Is my data safe?

In most cases, yes. A black screen doesn't erase your photos or messages, and replacing the screen doesn't touch your data either.

If you use iCloud Backup on iPhone, your phone backs up on its own when it's connected to power and Wi-Fi and locked. So leaving it plugged in at home overnight may give you a fresh backup even with a black screen.

When it's time for a repair

If you've tried a force restart, charging, brightness and the Screen Curtain fix, and the screen is still black while the phone is on, it's very likely a hardware problem with the display. A technician can confirm this, usually quickly, and tell you whether a screen replacement will fix it before any work starts.
