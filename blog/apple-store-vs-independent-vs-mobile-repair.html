Apple Store vs. independent repair vs. mobile repair: which is best?

Cracked screen, dying battery, broken charging port: where should you take your phone? Here's an honest comparison of your three options, so you can pick the one that fits your phone, your budget and your schedule.

When your phone breaks, you have three main choices in the Alexandria area: the Apple Store (or an Apple-authorized service provider), an independent repair shop, or a mobile repair service that comes to you. Each one has real pros and cons. Here's an honest look at all three.

Apple Store (and Apple-authorized service providers)

Good for: people with AppleCare+, and anyone who wants genuine Apple parts and Apple's own repair process.

  • Pros: genuine Apple parts, repairs done to Apple's standards, and a much lower price for screen damage if you have AppleCare+.
  • Cons: without AppleCare+, it's usually the most expensive option. You often need an appointment, you have to travel to the store (the nearest Apple Stores to Alexandria are in the Arlington and Tysons areas), and some repairs mean leaving your phone or mailing it in.

Honest tip: if your phone has AppleCare+, check with Apple first. For a cracked screen, AppleCare+ is often the best deal available.

Independent repair shops

Good for: people who want a lower price and don't mind going to a store.

  • Pros: usually cheaper than Apple, often same-day, they fix many brands and older models, and you can often choose between screen qualities (see LCD vs OLED screens).
  • Cons: quality varies a lot from shop to shop. Parts, warranties and pricing aren't standard, so you need to ask. You still have to drive there, and you may wait in the store or leave your phone for a few hours or days.

Mobile (on-site) repair

Good for: busy people, parents, and anyone who doesn't want to give up their day (or their phone) for a repair.

  • Pros: the technician comes to your home, office or a coffee shop. There's no driving, parking or waiting room, and your phone never leaves your sight, which many people like for privacy. Many mobile services also offer evening and weekend appointments.
  • Cons: you need to schedule a time instead of walking in. Some complex problems, like serious liquid damage, may need more time or equipment than an on-site visit allows. A good mobile technician will tell you upfront.

Which one is right for you?

  • You have AppleCare+: start with Apple.
  • You want the lowest possible price and don't mind the trip: compare a few well-reviewed independent shops, and ask which screen type each quote includes.
  • Your time matters, you have kids or a busy job, or you don't want to hand your phone to a stranger: a mobile repair service is usually the easiest option.

One more thing to know: in the US, having your phone repaired by an independent technician doesn't automatically cancel your Apple warranty, but Apple won't cover damage that was caused by a third-party repair. That's another reason to choose a repair service you trust.

5 questions to ask any repair service

  1. What's the total price, upfront? Get it before any work starts.
  2. What type of part is it? Genuine, refurbished original, aftermarket OLED or LCD.
  3. What does the warranty cover, and for how long?
  4. How long will the repair take?
  5. Does my phone have to leave my sight?
