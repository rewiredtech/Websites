FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you really come to me?

Yes. We're a fully mobile repair service. We come to your home, office or favorite coffee shop, anywhere from Washington, DC to Woodbridge, VA and from Fairfax, VA to Oxon Hill, MD, and repair your device on-site.

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on your device and what needs fixing. Send us your model and the problem, and we'll give you a free, upfront quote before any work begins. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. All parts we install and our labor are covered by a 60-day warranty against defects.

Which devices do you repair?

iPhone, Samsung Galaxy and other Android phones, plus supported Windows laptops and Apple MacBooks. If you're not sure we can fix yours, just ask.

What are your hours? Do you do same-day repairs?

We're available 7 days a week, 7 AM–10 PM, including weekends. For urgent repairs, call us and we'll do our best to fit you in the same day.

Will I lose my data?

Hardware repairs like screen and battery replacements usually don't affect your data. Still, we always recommend backing up your phone before any repair, just in case.
